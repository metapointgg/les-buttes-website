    eyebrow: Why these causes matter to us
    title: A legacy of community, care and second chances
    text: >-
      Martin and Josephine restored Les Buttes, raised their family here and were deeply rooted in Guernsey life. Mum lived with multiple sclerosis, while Dad lived with a heart condition for much of his life. Les Bourgs Hospice provided Mum with invaluable respite care and, in doing so, gave Dad vital support as her carer.

      Dad also believed strongly in giving people another chance. Through his building firm he supported people returning to the community after prison, including by offering employment, and his work as a prison inspector kept him closely connected with rehabilitation and life after release.

      The causes on this page are therefore personal to us. They reflect people and organisations whose work connects directly with our family's experience and with the values we want Les Buttes to carry forward.

    title: CLIP Guernsey
    text: >-
      CLIP — Creative Learning in Prison — supports prisoners in Guernsey through education, practical learning and work that helps prepare people for life back in the community.

      That has a particular resonance for our family. Dad supported people in prison and after release, sometimes by giving them employment in his building firm, because he believed people should not be defined by their worst moment.

      We are also asking CLIP to supply the garden benches at Les Buttes. They will be places for our guests to sit and enjoy the gardens, but they will also carry a little of that idea with them: useful things made through purposeful work and rehabilitation. When the benches are in place, we plan to add photographs here.

      Alongside individual fundraising events and practical support for local causes, we are looking at how charitable giving can become a regular part of Les Buttes.
