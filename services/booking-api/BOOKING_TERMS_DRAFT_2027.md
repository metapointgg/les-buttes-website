## 1. Booking request, acceptance and confirmation

A direct-booking enquiry is **a request, not an accepted reservation**. The owner will check availability in both the Les Buttes master calendar and the relevant Airbnb host calendar, occupancy/cottage capacity and any minimum-stay restrictions before accepting. A provisional hold or owner-reserved date block does not establish receipt of payment.

If a request is approved, Les Buttes will provide the exact cottage(s), arrival/departure dates, guest count, **complete agreed GBP price**, cancellation terms and a secure SumUp payment link, with a clear time limit for paying. No payment should be requested until the guest has been able to read and expressly accept the **final approved** terms.

The standard intended payment is the **full agreed amount**, paid using the owner-issued secure link. A prospective booking becomes confirmed only after the owner independently verifies receipt and amount of payment and issues written confirmation. Until then it is **RESERVED / awaiting completion**, not paid/confirmed. No auto-debit, deposits, instalments or extra payment charges are implied.

## 2. Guest names, occupancy and use

The guest making the booking must provide accurate contact details, ages/number of guests if required for permit compliance, and identify any needs affecting the property. Overnight occupancy must not exceed the limits confirmed with the cottage. Guests must use the accommodation responsibly, respect neighbours and comply with the separately supplied and accepted house rules.

The host will disclose material restrictions, safety information, access arrangements and available amenities before acceptance. Any additional guest, event or substantive date changes require host approval and written agreement on any revised price or policy.

## 3. Pricing and payment

The owner-agreed written total is the accommodation price payable. It may be lower than the equivalent Airbnb guest-facing calendar quotation through an individually negotiated direct offer. **There is no guaranteed discount percentage**, and Airbnb's platform protection, payment or cancellation processes do not apply.

The guest must see a single unambiguous total, the component prices for each cottage when both are booked, any compulsory extras, and any applicable taxes, **before** paying. The host must not subsequently increase the price without the guest's express agreement. SumUp processes card payments; Les Buttes must independently check the successful payment in the merchant account before confirming.

## 4. Check-in, care and problems during the stay

Arrival and departure instructions, parking, keys and a local point of contact will be provided before travel. Guests should promptly report any safety hazard, defect, damage or material difference from the advertised property so that Les Buttes can investigate and remedy the situation.

These terms do **not** exclude or limit rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded, including any rights arising where the agreed accommodation is not supplied or is materially misdescribed. Any liability wording needs legal review before publication.

## 5. Requests to change a booking

Changes to cottages, dates, guest count or other material terms require written agreement. Availability and price will be rechecked before any amended arrangement is accepted. An enquiry to change a booking does not itself cancel or modify the existing booking.

## 6. Guest cancellation and refunds — PROPOSED FIRMLIKE POLICY (NOT APPROVED)

**The exact times must be confirmed before this clause can be used or coded.** Cancellation deadlines are measured backwards from the agreed local check-in time on the arrival date, using **Europe/Guernsey** time. The cancellation notice must be sent to the verified booking contact channel in writing; the date/time received will be acknowledged by Les Buttes.

Subject to consumer law and other specific written agreements:

1. **24-hour initial cancellation period:** if the booking has been confirmed **at least seven full days** before check-in, the guest can cancel within **24 hours of receiving written booking confirmation** for a **100% refund of the agreed booking amount**.
2. **30-day refund period:** otherwise, cancellation **at least 30 full days before check-in** yields a **100% refund**.
3. **7–30 days before check-in:** cancellation **at least seven full days but fewer than 30 full days** before check-in yields a **50% refund**.
4. **Under seven days / after check-in:** no ordinary discretionary cancellation refund is offered, subject to statutory rights, host fault, agreed exceptional circumstances or a separate written concession.
5. The initial 24-hour rule takes priority when applicable. A guest's refund is never more than actually paid. All percentages apply to the **agreed full guest price**, with any separately itemised compulsory fees/taxes handled explicitly and lawfully; **no invented non-refundable card fee** will be withheld.
6. Les Buttes may choose to offer additional goodwill refunds in writing, without changing the underlying policy for other guests.

This is a **proposed direct policy**, based broadly on Airbnb's **current Firm short-stay structure**, *not* the obsolete standard Strict policy. These Airbnb policy terms are not automatically incorporated into off-platform contracts.

**Legislation note:** Section 85(1)(i) of the **Trading Standards (Fair Trading) (Guernsey) Ordinance, 2023** as published in the States' 2023 legislative materials excludes accommodation supplied for specified dates from the general distance-contract cancellation chapter. Confirm this provision in the **currently enacted/consolidated text** and review all other consumer protections and unfair-term restrictions with Guernsey-qualified advisers before issuing an enforceable policy. A specific-date accommodation exception is **not** a general waiver of consumer rights or compensation for host breach.

## 7. Host cancellation and failure to provide the booked accommodation

If Les Buttes cannot supply the agreed cottage(s) for the booked dates, the host must promptly notify the guest, provide an appropriate **full refund** of sums paid for accommodation not provided, and consider reasonable assistance/alternatives if the guest wishes. Nothing in this clause excludes any additional rights or remedies the guest may have under applicable law. Alternative accommodation must not be imposed without agreement.

Where a material property defect or serious disruption makes performance impossible, resolve it fairly with the guest with regard to applicable law, evidence and available remedies. Travel disruption, weather, ill health, major events and rebooking outcomes require a clearly defined, separately approved approach before final publication; do not assume Airbnb's Major Disruptive Events Policy applies directly.

## 8. Cancellation and refund administration

All cancellation requests and any exceptions are **reviewed manually**. Prior to any refund, the owner records the original guest amount, payment status, cancellation timestamp in Guernsey local time, applicable policy version, refund calculation, and any agreed adjustments.

Refunds should normally return through the original payment method using verified SumUp merchant controls. Payment-link status, chargebacks and actual refunds must be checked in SumUp; **updating the AWS booking status is not itself a refund**. Do not unblock dates or mark a booking cancelled until the owner's review and required refund/guest communications have been recorded. The current admin CLI needs dedicated operational guardrails before it can safely support all these cases.

## 9. Privacy and use of booking information

Les Buttes will use guest enquiry and reservation details to respond to enquiries, assess availability, administer and fulfil bookings, process payments through SumUp and comply with legal obligations. The final privacy notice must identify the responsible data controller, data categories, legal grounds, processors (including AWS infrastructure and any email services), retention and overseas transfers as applicable, data-subject rights and the verified contact for enquiries.

The **Data Protection (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 2017** applies. Do not collect payment-card details by email, store them in the master booking record, or include private Airbnb iCalendar tokens in any guest material.

## 10. Confirmation and policy-version evidence

Once owner/legal review has approved the final terms, every guest should receive the same version **before being asked to pay**, with an explicit method to accept it. The owner should record **terms version, date/time presented, acknowledgement timestamp/method, booking reference, agreed sum and applicable cancellation cutoff dates**.

Send a durable copy of the accepted terms with the payment request and retain a copy of the guest confirmation. Never send a SumUp payment link from an unapproved terms draft.
